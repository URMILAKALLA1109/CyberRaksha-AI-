HOW CYBERRAKSHA WORKS

From confusion to action.

A simple four-step process designed to help citizens respond after a cyber incident.

01

Tell Us

Type or speak what happened in your own language.

02

Get Guidance

Receive immediate actions relevant to the incident.

03

Preserve Evidence

Get a checklist of screenshots, transaction IDs, calls and messages.

04

Prepare & Report

Organize the incident clearly and access official reporting channels.

ℹ️

Important

CyberRaksha AI is a citizen-support prototype. It helps organize information and provides guidance. For official complaints and reporting, always use authorized government channels.
